From Marcus's classical electrostatics and dielectric continuum models, to representing collective solvent motions as a bath of harmonic oscillators; from the fluctuation–dissipation theorem and Langevin equations, which describe the solvent's influence on an electron-transfer center through random forces and friction, to stochastic density functional theory (SDFT), ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD), and neural-network-based molecular dynamics.

Meanwhile, SFG and SHS spectroscopy allow us to probe water's molecular orientation and hydrogen-bond correlations.

I think it is time to revisit and bring together our understanding of the relationship between solvents and electron transfer, particularly in the adiabatic electron-transfer regime.....
